# Delegated authority matrix

**Kestrel Pay Ltd and Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd. Version 4, approved by the board on 10 December 2025, in force from 1 January 2026. Owner: Helen Marsh, General Counsel.** It replaces version 3, which was in force from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.

The matrix says who may commit the company, and up to what amount. If a decision is not covered, ask Legal before you commit to anything.

## General rules

1. **Whole value.** Limits apply to the total value of a commitment over its whole term, excluding VAT. A 3-year contract at £100,000 a year is a £300,000 contract.
2. **No splitting.** Splitting a commitment to bring it under a limit is a disciplinary matter.
3. **No self-approval.** Nobody may approve something they requested or that benefits them personally.
4. **Two signatories** means two different people, one of whom holds the level shown.
5. **Budget first.** Anything outside the approved budget needs the CFO's approval before it goes through the normal route, whatever its value.
6. **Cover.** When an approver is away, a deputy named in Legal's authority log may approve up to the same limit. Deputies cannot approve their own items.
7. **Executives** are the CEO (Amara Okafor), the CFO (Daniel Reyes), the CTO (Priya Nair) and the General Counsel (Helen Marsh).

## Table 1: Signing contracts

| Total contract value | Who may sign | Legal review |
|---|---|---|
| Up to £50,000 | Budget holder (head of department) | Only if not on a Kestrel template |
| £50,001 to £250,000 | One executive | Required |
| £250,001 to £5,000,000 | Two executives, one of whom is the CEO or the CFO | Required |
| Over £5,000,000 | Board approval first, then the CEO and the CFO | Required |
| Any lease longer than 5 years, acquisition or disposal of a business, borrowing, guarantee, or issue of shares | Board approval first, then the CEO and the CFO | Required |
| NDAs, data processing agreements, and amendments that do not change the value | General Counsel or Commercial Counsel | Legal signs |

Every signed contract goes to Legal within 5 working days to be logged in the contract register with a KPC number.

## Table 2: Paying suppliers

| Single invoice or payment, gross | Approval to pay |
|---|---|
| Up to £10,000 | AP Specialist |
| £10,000.01 to £50,000 | Financial Controller |
| £50,000.01 to £250,000 | CFO |
| Over £250,000 | CFO and CEO |

Every payment from a bank account needs two people in the bank portal: one to input and a different one to release. Payments to new or changed bank details follow the supplier payment run procedure.

## Table 3: People

| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Job offer within the salary band and the budget | Hiring manager and the People partner |
| Job offer above the band | Executive for the area and the Head of People |
| New role not in the budget | CFO and the executive for the area |
| Settlement agreement up to £25,000 | Head of People and the General Counsel |
| Settlement agreement over £25,000 | CEO and the General Counsel |
| Any change to an executive's pay | Remuneration Committee |

## Table 4: Commercial and finance

| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Discount up to 15% off list price | Head of Sales |
| Discount over 15%, or any non-standard pricing term | CFO |
| Write-off of merchant debt up to £5,000 | Head of Operations or delegate |
| Write-off from £5,001 to £50,000 | Financial Controller |
| Write-off over £50,000 | CFO |
| Capital spending in the budget, up to £100,000 | Budget holder |
| Capital spending over £100,000, or not in the budget | CFO |
| Opening or closing a bank account | CFO and one other executive |
| Treasury deposits of own cash, up to 12 months, with approved banks | CFO |

## Table 5: Legal and regulatory

| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Regulatory returns and routine submissions | Compliance Officer prepares, General Counsel approves |
| Notifying the FCA or the Central Bank of Ireland of a breach or significant event | CEO and General Counsel |
| Starting legal proceedings | General Counsel and CEO |
| Settling a claim up to £25,000 | General Counsel |
| Settling a claim from £25,001 to £250,000 | General Counsel and CEO |
| Settling a claim over £250,000 | Board |
| Press statements and public comment | CEO, or a person the CEO names |

## Changes from version 3

- The AP Specialist's payment limit rises from £5,000 to £10,000.
- Two executives must now sign contracts from £250,001; the threshold was £500,001.
- Leases longer than 5 years now need board approval, whatever their value.
- Data processing agreements can now be signed by the Commercial Counsel as well as the General Counsel.

## Records

Legal keeps the authority log, which lists deputies and any one-off delegations the board or the CEO has made. Internal Audit tests a sample of contracts and payments against this matrix every six months and reports to the Audit and Risk Committee.
