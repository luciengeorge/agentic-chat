# FY2026 budget: notes and assumptions

**Prepared by Jonas Berg (FP&A Manager) with Mia Chen and Chris Dunn. Version 3 (final), 11 December 2025. Approved by the board on 10 December 2025.**

These notes explain how the FY2026 budget was built, so that budget holders and the finance team read the numbers the same way. The model itself is in this folder (FY2026 Budget Model v3.xlsx). FY2026 runs from 1 January to 31 December 2026.

**Update, 21 January 2026.** FY2025 unaudited actuals are now in: revenue £138.4m, gross margin 56.2%, EBITDA £3.0m, 45,620 merchant customers and 562 employees at 31 December 2025. The budget is unchanged. Against the actual, budgeted revenue growth is 17.0%.

## 3. Gross margin

**Quarterly profit, £000**

| Line | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 37,800 | 39,700 | 41,500 | 43,000 | 162,000 |
| Gross profit | 21,735 | 22,950 | 24,280 | 25,319 | 94,284 |
| Gross margin | 57.5% | 57.8% | 58.5% | 58.9% | 58.2% |
| Operating costs | (20,045) | (20,900) | (21,900) | (22,839) | (85,684) |
| EBITDA | 1,690 | 2,050 | 2,380 | 2,480 | 8,600 |

The margin rises through the year for three reasons:

- **Hosting.** We assume a new hosting contract from 1 April 2026, under which we buy our AWS capacity through a reseller that also runs the production environment day to day. Ashgrove Cloud Services is the preferred bidder at £61,400 a month for 36 months, covering committed AWS capacity and 24/7 operations (£0.74m a year), plus about £0.2m a year of usage above the commitment. This is £0.6m a year cheaper than buying AWS directly and renewing the current managed service contract.
- **Fraud.** Fraud losses and chargebacks are budgeted at 3.1% of revenue, against 3.5% in FY2025, on the strength of the new Kestrel Shield scoring model planned for Q1.
- **Mix.** Payouts and Accounts carry better margins than Checkout, and both grow faster.

Interchange and scheme fees pass straight through and are budgeted at 50.4% of Checkout revenue, including the scheme fee increases of about 3% from April.

## 6. Capital spending and cash

- Capital expenditure £10.4m, of which £8.6m is capitalised development (about 30% of Engineering time).
- Depreciation and amortisation £5.8m.
- Budgeted cash at 31 December 2026: £68.5m. Customer funds held in safeguarding accounts are not included.

## 7. Other assumptions

- **FX:** EUR to GBP at 0.86 for the whole year.
- **Interest:** Bank Rate averaging 3.75% in 2026. We earn interest on our own cash only.
- **Pay:** 4.5% average rise from 1 April 2026; employer National Insurance at 15%.
- **No acquisitions.** The budget includes no M&A. Any deal would come to the board separately with its own funding.
- **Prices:** revenue is built on price list 2026.1, which takes effect on 1 January 2026, plus the changes agreed with Sales for 1 April 2026 (price list 2026.2, for new contracts and renewals): Checkout Pro monthly (KC-PRO-01) rises from £45 to £49 a month, extra expense cards (KA-CARD-01) from £2.50 to £3 a month, and the KP-BULK-2000 overage falls from 28p to 25p a payout. Because the April changes apply only to new contracts and renewals, together they add about £0.1m to FY2026 revenue. No other list price changes are assumed in 2026.

| Hiring runs 2 months late on average | none | +£1.8m |

## 9. Monthly reporting against the budget

- FP&A loads the budget into NetSuite by 9 January 2026, phased by month.
- Budget holders get a variance report on WD8 each month with the management accounts.
- Any variance over £100,000 or 10% on a cost line needs a one-line explanation from the budget holder by WD10.
- We reforecast the full year after each quarter close and take it to the next board meeting.
