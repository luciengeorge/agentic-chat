## 2. Safeguarding

Kestrel safeguards relevant funds by segregation, in designated safeguarding accounts at Albion Clearing Bank (GBP) and Banco Meridional, S.A. (EUR). At 30 June 2026 Kestrel Pay Ltd held £1.02bn of customer funds: £0.94bn in GBP and €95m (about £0.08bn) in EUR. Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd held a further £0.22bn, so the group total was £1.24bn.

The FCA's new safeguarding rules came into force on 7 May 2026. They add a monthly safeguarding return and require the resolution pack to be kept up to date. Finance updated the safeguarding reconciliation procedure to version 3.1 from the same date.

**Daily reconciliations.** There were 61 UK business days in Q2. All 61 reconciliations were finished by the 11:00 deadline, and none showed a shortfall.

**Q1 breach, now closed.** On Thursday 12 March the EUR reconciliation was finished at 11:20, after the deadline, because the reconciliation tool could not load a bank statement file after the bank changed its file format. There was no shortfall. We reported the breach to the FCA on 13 March (SG-2026-01). Treasury Operations now checks at 15:00 every business day that each bank's statement file has arrived.

**Monthly returns.** The first monthly safeguarding return (May) was submitted on 12 June. The June return was submitted on 14 July, ahead of the deadline of 21 July.

**Annual audit.** Fernside & Co LLP, our safeguarding auditor since October 2025, completed the audit for 2025 on 27 March 2026. It gave a reasonable assurance opinion with two low-rated findings, both closed by 30 April. Interim fieldwork for the 2026 audit (KPC-2026-0326) has been brought forward to 7 to 25 September 2026.

The EDD backlog grew because two analysts left in April. Three contractors start on 1 July and the target is fewer than 50 overdue reviews by 30 September (action CM-26-11). No high-risk merchant has been left unreviewed for more than 60 days past its review date.

Identity checks for merchant onboarding run through Sorrel Identity Ltd (KPC-2025-0230). Its Q2 service review found no issues.

A card-testing attack on Kestrel Checkout in April caused £180k of chargeback losses before new fraud rules stopped it. It is covered in the fraud report to the Audit and Risk Committee.

## 4. Complaints

| Measure | Q1 2026 | Q2 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Complaints received | 432 | 432 |
| Complaints per 1,000 merchants | 9.2 | 9.0 |
| Resolved within 15 business days | 93.5% | 96.8% |
| Referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service | 9 | 11 |
| Ombudsman decisions against Kestrel | 2 | 3 |

Main causes in Q2: payout delays (31%), chargeback disputes (22%), account closures (14%), fees and pricing (11%) and other issues (22%). Payout delay complaints rose in June. Two of the three Ombudsman decisions against Kestrel concerned account closures where our letters gave too little reason; Legal and Operations are rewriting the closure letters. The third, on 21 April (CMP-2026-01171), concerned international transfer fees charged twice in March; we refunded £3,850.00 to the 412 Kestrel Accounts customers affected and fixed the fee rule on 24 April.

## 5. Financial promotions

We reviewed 38 financial promotions in Q2: 36 were approved and 2 were rejected because the fee wording was unclear. On 9 April a LinkedIn post about Kestrel Accounts went out before Compliance had approved it. It was taken down within three hours and no complaint was received (breach B-2026-05). Training for Marketing and Sales is booked for August (action CM-26-14).

## 6. Operational resilience and outsourcing

- There were no P1 incidents in Q2, so nothing was reportable under the incident reporting rules. All P2 and P3 incidents stayed within the impact tolerances for our important business services.
- In Q1 we reported five incidents to the FCA: four P1 incidents and major incident MI-2026-03 on 6 February, when an expired TLS certificate on the link to the card processor caused 41 minutes of declined card payments.
- Ashgrove Cloud Services took over the running of our AWS production environment on 1 April, as planned, under KPC-2026-0142. We notified the FCA of this material outsourcing on 20 February 2026.
- The annual operational resilience self-assessment was approved by the board in March 2026.

## 7. Data protection

On 3 June a monthly statement for one merchant was emailed to another merchant because of a wrong address in the CRM. The statement showed the business name, account number and transactions; no card numbers. We assessed the risk to individuals as low and did not report it to the ICO. Both merchants were told, and the recipient confirmed deletion (breach B-2026-06). We answered all 14 subject access requests within one month.
