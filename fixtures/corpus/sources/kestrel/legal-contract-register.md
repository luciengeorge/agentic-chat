# Contract register

**Kestrel Pay Ltd and Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd. Export from the contract system on 28 September 2026.** Maintained by Maya Levi (Paralegal). Owner: Helen Marsh (General Counsel).

## How to read this register

- **What is listed.** Every active contract worth £25,000 a year or more, every lease, every data processing agreement and every outsourcing arrangement we must report to a regulator. NDAs and order forms under £25,000 are held in the contract system but not listed here, unless the General Counsel asks for a restricted entry.
- **Numbers.** Contract numbers take the form KPC-YYYY-NNNN. Legal issues them in sequence when it opens the contract matter, so the year is the year the matter was opened, which can differ from the signing date or the start date. From 1 January 2026 Legal also logs NDAs, order forms and amendments, which is why 2026 numbers run higher than earlier years. Always quote the full number: KPC-2026-0142 and KPC-2025-0142 are different contracts, and so are KPC-2026-0142 and KPC-2026-0412.
- **Owner** is the Kestrel budget holder responsible for the contract. **(KPE)** marks a contract held by Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd.
- **Renewal.** Auto: renews unless notice is given. Fixed: ends on the end date. Evergreen: no end date; ends on notice.
- **Annual value** excludes VAT. Restricted entries show only the number, type, owner and dates; ask the General Counsel for details.
- Dates are day/month/year.
