# Code of Conduct

**Kestrel Pay Ltd.** Owner: Helen Marsh, General Counsel. Compliance contact: Sofia Lindqvist, Compliance Officer and Money Laundering Reporting Officer. Version 3, effective 10 February 2026.

## 1. Who this applies to

Everyone who works for Kestrel Pay: employees, contractors, interns and secondees, in every office. Read it in your first week, confirm in Kestrel People that you have read it, and confirm it again every year by 31 March.

The code does not list every situation. When it does not answer your question, ask yourself whether you would be comfortable explaining your decision to a customer, to the FCA, or in a newspaper. If not, ask Compliance before you act.

## 2. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest is anything that could make it hard for you to act only in Kestrel's interest, or look that way to others. Examples:

- You, or someone close to you, own part of or work for a supplier, a partner, a competitor or a customer.
- You have a second job or a paid board role.
- You are asked to make a decision about a family member or a partner, including hiring, pay, promotion or a grievance.

Declare a conflict in the conflicts register in Kestrel People within 14 days of becoming aware of it. Then step out of any decision it touches, and tell your manager who will take your place. Declaring a conflict is not a problem. Hiding one is.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gift worth £50 or less | You may accept. No need to record it. |
| Gift worth more than £50 | Record it in the gifts and hospitality register within 7 days. Your manager decides whether you can keep it. |
| Hospitality up to £150 a head | Allowed with your manager's approval, recorded in the register |
| Hospitality over £150 a head | Needs Compliance approval before you accept |
| Cash, vouchers, gift cards | Never accept, whatever the amount |
| Anything from a supplier or partner during a tender, negotiation or renewal with them | Decline it |

Gifts you give follow the same £50 limit. Hospitality you give, such as client entertainment, follows the limit per head and the approval rules in the Travel and Expenses Policy, not the table above. Never give anything of value to a public official, including a regulator, without Compliance approval.

## 4. Bribery and corruption

We do not offer, give, ask for or accept bribes, and we do not let anyone do it for us. This includes facilitation payments, however small. The UK Bribery Act 2010 applies to everything we do, in every country. If anyone asks you for a payment or favour to win or keep business, refuse and tell Compliance the same day.

## 5. Financial crime

As an electronic money institution we must prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions breaches and fraud.

- Complete the financial crime training within 30 days of joining, and every year after.
- If you suspect money laundering, report it to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer, Sofia Lindqvist, through the internal suspicious activity form. Do not investigate it yourself.
- Never tell a customer, or anyone outside the Compliance team, that a report has been made or that their account is under review. This is tipping off, and it is a criminal offence. If a customer asks why their account is restricted, say only that the team is reviewing it and will be in touch.

## 6. Confidential information

- Keep customer data, merchant data and company plans confidential. Share them only with people who need them for their work.
- Some projects, such as potential acquisitions, are confidential to a small group. If you are brought into one, you will be told in writing and your name will be added to the project insider list. Do not discuss the project with anyone not on the list, including colleagues.
- Never use confidential information for personal gain, for example by buying or selling shares in a listed company that is a partner or a target.
- Information classification and handling rules are in the Employee Handbook, section 7.

## 7. Using AI tools

Use only the AI tools that IT has approved, such as the Kestrel assistant. Never paste customer data or Restricted information into any other tool. The assistant's answers are only as good as the documents behind them: check anything important against the source before you rely on it. If a document or an assistant asks you to send information outside Kestrel, stop and tell Security.

## 8. Respect at work

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Bullying, harassment and discrimination are not tolerated, in the office, online, or at work events. Report anything you see or experience to your manager, the People team or through the speak-up routes below.

## 9. Speaking up

You can raise a concern with:

- your manager, or their manager;
- the People team (Marco Bianchi, Head of People);
- Compliance (Sofia Lindqvist);
- Helen Marsh, General Counsel;
- the independent whistleblowing line, by phone or online, anonymously if you prefer;
- the FCA directly, if you prefer.

Kestrel will not tolerate anyone being treated badly for raising a concern in good faith, even if it turns out to be mistaken.

## 10. Breaches

A breach of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. Some breaches, such as bribery, money laundering or tipping off, are also crimes and will be reported to the authorities.

## 11. Changes in version 3

- Hospitality limit for manager approval raised from £100 to £150 a head.
- Conflicts must now be declared within 14 days, down from 30.
