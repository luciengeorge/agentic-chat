## 1. Summary

| Measure | Q3 2026 | Q3 2025 | Change | YTD 2026 | YTD budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | £41.2m | £35.2m | +17.0% | £118.3m | £119.0m |
| Gross margin | 58.0% | 56.1% | +1.9 pts | 57.5% | 58.0% |
| EBITDA | £2.1m | £0.9m | +£1.2m | £5.4m | £6.1m |
| Merchant customers at quarter end | 49,870 | 43,950 | +13.5% | | |
| Headcount at quarter end | 604 | 548 | +56 | | |

- **Revenue was £41.2m**, up 17.0% on Q3 2025 and 4.3% on Q2 2026. It was £313k (0.8%) below the budget of £41.5m: Checkout and Payouts were slightly behind in July and August, and September beat budget once the two payroll bureaus went live on Payouts on 1 September.
- **Gross margin was 58.0%**, up 0.6 points on Q2 and 1.9 points on a year ago, and 0.5 points below budget. Fraud losses (£83k over budget) and hosting usage above our committed capacity (£36k over) explain most of the gap.
- **EBITDA was £2.1m** (5.2% of revenue) against a budget of £2.4m. Operating costs were £145k under budget: staff costs were £310k under because hiring is behind plan, and professional fees were £305k over.
- **Headcount was 604** against a budget of 618. Following the exec decision of 11 September, non-critical backfills are paused from 1 October, and we now forecast 625 at year end against a budget of 640.
- **Cash was £70.1m** at 30 September (£71.3m at 30 June). This excludes customer funds, which are held in safeguarding accounts.
- **Full-year forecast:** revenue £161.0m (budget £162.0m) and EBITDA £7.7m (budget £8.6m).

**Commentary on the income statement**

- **Cost of revenue** was £79k over budget. Fraud losses and chargebacks were £83k over, mostly two merchant fraud cases in August (£61k, of which £12k has been recovered so far). Processing and hosting was £36k over because Payouts traffic in September pushed usage above the committed capacity in our hosting contract with Ashgrove Cloud Services (KPC-2026-0142). Interchange and scheme fees were £60k under, in line with lower Checkout volume.
- **Staff costs** were £310k under budget. Average headcount in the quarter was 597 against a budgeted 608.
- **Professional fees** were £305k over budget, for three reasons: interim fieldwork for the 2026 safeguarding audit by Fernside & Co LLP (£140k, KPC-2026-0326), brought forward from Q4; legal fees for the Dublin office lease (£85k); and EU VAT and OSS registration advice from Ostrander Tax Advisers LLP (£80k, KPC-2026-0301).
- **Sales and marketing** rose £180k on Q2 because of the September merchant acquisition campaign in Ireland, but stayed £40k under budget.
- **Net finance income** is interest on Kestrel's own cash. It does not include any return on customer funds.

## 7. Notes and definitions

- **Checkout revenue** is the merchant service charge, before interchange and scheme fees, which are shown in cost of revenue.
- **Accounts revenue** is monthly account fees, interchange earned on Kestrel debit cards, and FX margin.
- **Payouts revenue** is per-payment fees and FX margin on supplier and payroll payouts.
- **Merchant customer:** a business with at least one Kestrel product live and at least one transaction or fee in the last 90 days.
- **Headcount:** permanent and fixed-term employees on payroll at the date. It excludes 23 contractors and 6 interns.
- **Take rate:** Checkout revenue divided by Checkout payment volume.
- **Budget:** the FY2026 budget approved by the board on 10 December 2025.
