## Item 3: CEO report (B26/07/02)

- **Merchants.** 48,210 merchant customers at 30 June, up 1,330 in the quarter (46,880 at 31 March) and up 13.3% on a year ago (42,540).
- **Products.** EUR balances in Kestrel Accounts, open to UK merchants since 12 January, were used by 2,140 merchants by 30 June. Payouts is the fastest-growing product, though two payroll bureaus moved their go-live from May to 1 September. That cost about £0.4m of Q2 revenue against budget, half of it made up by other Payouts customers.
- **People.** Headcount was 589 at 30 June against a plan of 598. Engineering hiring in Lisbon is behind by six roles.
- **Incidents.** No P1 incidents in the quarter, so nothing was reportable to the FCA. A card-testing attack on Checkout in April added £180k of chargeback losses before new rules stopped it.
- **Priorities for H2.** Payouts bureau go-lives on 1 September, the Dublin move, clearing the EDD backlog, and Project Heron if the board approves item 5.

## Item 5: Project Heron (B26/07/04)

Project Heron is the proposed acquisition of Lumen Ledger, Lda, a Lisbon bookkeeping and invoicing software company with about €9m of ARR and 6,000 SME customers in Portugal and Spain. The mutual NDA was signed on 10 June (KPC-2026-0298). The full evaluation paper is in the reading room.

**Proposal.** Management asks the board to approve:

1. a non-binding indicative offer for 100% of Lumen Ledger, Lda at an enterprise value of £86m, cash-free and debt-free, with part of the price in Kestrel shares and an earn-out;
2. exclusivity with the sellers until 30 November 2026;
3. a due diligence budget of up to £650k, with Carrow Finch LLP (UK legal), Almeida Reis & Associados (Portuguese legal) and Hartwell Moss LLP (financial and tax).

If approved, the offer letter goes on 24 July. Due diligence would finish in mid-September and a final recommendation would come to the board on 20 October 2026.

**Confidentiality.** This item is restricted to the board and the exec team. Helen Marsh holds the insider list.
