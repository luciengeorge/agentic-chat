# Pricing FAQ for Sales and Customer Success

Maintained by Chloe Dubois (Account Executive) with Jake Morrison (Head of Sales). Last updated 2 September 2026. Every price here comes from price list 2026.2, effective 1 April 2026. If the two ever disagree, the price list wins; tell Chloe so this FAQ can be fixed.

## Plans and SKUs

**What is the difference between KC-STD-01 and KC-STD-12?**
Both are Checkout Standard with no monthly fee. KC-STD-01 is monthly rolling at 1.49% + 20p for UK and EEA consumer cards. KC-STD-12 is a 12-month term at 1.39% + 20p. Non-UK, non-EEA and commercial cards are 2.49% + 20p on KC-STD-01 and 2.39% + 20p on KC-STD-12.

**When should a merchant move to Checkout Pro?**
Checkout Pro costs £49 a month on KC-PRO-01 or £39 a month on KC-PRO-12, and charges 1.19% + 15p or 1.09% + 15p. On UK consumer cards, KC-PRO-12 is cheaper than KC-STD-12 once a merchant processes about £9,200 a month at an average ticket of £40. Pro also brings the 99.95% availability commitment, same-day settlement to a Kestrel Accounts balance and the £20,000 single transaction limit.

**Why did KC-PRO-01 go up?**
It rose from £45 to £49 a month on 1 April 2026 (price list 2026.2). Existing KC-PRO-01 merchants kept £45 until 1 July 2026 and were told by email on 2 March 2026. KC-PRO-12 stayed at £39.

**Can a merchant switch from monthly to a 12-month plan mid-month?**
Yes. The 12-month term starts on the day of the switch, and the first invoice is pro-rated. A move the other way, from 12 months to monthly, counts as ending the 12-month plan early.

**What does it cost to leave a 12-month plan early?**
The remaining monthly fees for the term, capped at £300 or €350 per plan. KC-STD-12 has no monthly fee, so leaving it early costs nothing; the merchant simply goes back to KC-STD-01 rates.

## Terminals

**Rent or buy a terminal?**
KC-TERM-01 rents a Kestrel Tap T2 for £15 a month per device, with free replacement. KC-TERM-BUY is a one-off £149 per device with a 12-month warranty. Buying pays back after 10 months, so recommend buying to merchants who expect to stay longer than a year.

**Can we give terminal rental free?**
Up to 3 free months of KC-TERM-01 per site, only for merchants signing KC-PRO-12, and only with Head of Sales approval.

## Accounts and Payouts

**Is KA-PLUS-12 billed monthly?**
No. KA-PLUS-12 is £12 a month billed yearly in advance, so the merchant pays £144 (or €168) at the start of the term. KA-PLUS-01 is £14 a month billed monthly.

**How much is an extra card?**
KA-CARD-01 is £3 (€3.50) a month per card beyond the plan's allowance. It was £2.50 before 1 April 2026.

**What happens when a KP-BULK-500 merchant sends more than 500 payouts?**
The first 500 payouts each month are covered by the £125 fee. Each extra payout costs 30p (€0.35). Unused payouts do not roll over. If a merchant regularly sends more than about 1,420 payouts a month, KP-BULK-2000 at £400 is cheaper.

**What about pay as you go?**
KP-PAYG-01 has no monthly fee and charges 35p per UK payout or €0.45 per SEPA payout. It is cheaper than KP-BULK-500 below 357 payouts a month.

**Can merchants pay suppliers abroad?**
Yes, with the KP-FX-01 add-on: 0.6% above the mid-market rate, minimum £2 per payout.

**Is KP-BULK-500 the same as error KP-500?**
No. SKUs always have letters after the prefix (KP-BULK-500). Payments API error codes are four digits (for example KP-5003). There is no code KP-500.

## Discounts

**How much can I discount?**
On monthly fees, an Account Executive can give up to 10%, the Head of Sales up to 15%, and anything above 15% needs the CFO, as the Delegated Authority Matrix sets out. Any transaction rate below the plan rate is a non-standard pricing term: it goes through deal desk to the CFO. Nothing goes below the floor of 0.95% + 10p.

**Do we price-match?**
Not automatically. If a merchant shows a written competing offer, take it to deal desk with their last 3 months of card volume. Deal desk answers within 2 business days.

**What do Brightwell Payroll referrals get?**
20% off KP-BULK-500 or KP-BULK-2000 for the first 3 months. This is a partner price, approved by the CFO as part of the Brightwell agreement, so it does not use your discount authority. Tag the opportunity with the Brightwell referral source.

## Fees customers ask about

**Are there set-up fees?**
No, on any plan.

**What is the chargeback fee?**
£15 (€17) per chargeback, refunded if the merchant wins the dispute.

**Do refunds cost anything?**
Refunds are free, but the original transaction fee is not returned.

**What is the Checkout Pro availability commitment?**
99.95% monthly availability. Below that, affected Checkout Pro merchants receive a service credit on the next invoice: 10% of the monthly fee for availability from 99.0% to below 99.95%, 25% from 95.0% to below 99.0%, and 50% below 95.0%. After the incident on 17 August 2026, August availability was 99.89%, so affected Pro merchants received the 10% credit. Customer Success handles credits; see the Customer Success Escalation Guide.
