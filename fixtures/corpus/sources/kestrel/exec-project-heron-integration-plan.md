## 1. Principles

- Lumen keeps its product name, its Lisbon and Porto teams and its customer contracts for at least 12 months.
- Customers see no change on Day 1 apart from a letter.
- Lumen moves to Kestrel's finance, legal, security and people policies by Day 100.
- One budget and one steering group. Workstreams do not spend outside the integration budget without the CFO's approval.

## 4. Finance workstream (Tom Whitfield)

1. **Completion accounts.** Hartwell Moss prepares the completion balance sheet as at 30 November 2026. Buyer and sellers must agree it within 60 days, so by 29 January 2027. Any cash or debt adjustment is settled within 10 working days after that.
2. **Purchase price allocation.** Hartwell Moss delivers it by 28 February 2027. Working estimates: cost of the business combination £83.9m (cash £54.0m, consideration shares £20.0m, earn-out at an estimated fair value of £8.5m, deal costs £1.4m); intangible assets about £19m (customer relationships and software); goodwill about £61m. We propose to amortise goodwill over 10 years, about £6.1m a year.
3. **Earn-out.** Track Lumen ARR each month from January 2027, using the SPA definition (no contracts under 12 months, no discounts above 20%). Report it to the steering group monthly. Reassess the earn-out's fair value at each year end. The measurement date is 31 December 2027 and any payment falls in Q2 2028.
4. **Consolidation.** Lumen Ledger, Lda becomes a subsidiary of Kestrel Pay Ltd. It keeps its Portuguese statutory accounts under SNC; we map its trial balance to the Kestrel chart of accounts each month. The first consolidated month is December 2026. Balance sheet items translate at the month-end EUR rate and the income statement at the monthly average.
5. **Close.** Lumen's outsourced accountant, Contabilidade Tejo, Lda, delivers the trial balance by WD4. From the December 2026 close, which runs in early January 2027, Lumen sits on WD5 of the Kestrel close calendar.
6. **Banking.** Lumen's accounts at Banco Ondina stay open for at least 6 months. On Day 1 Tom Whitfield and Daniel Reyes become signatories; Inês Carvalho stays a signatory up to €25,000 a payment.
7. **Facility.** The £30m Tamar Bank term loan is drawn on Day 1 at 08:00. Covenants are a minimum cash balance of £25m at all times and net leverage below 3.0 times, tested quarterly from 31 March 2027. Tom Whitfield prepares the compliance certificates.
8. **Payroll.** Lumen stays on its own Portuguese payroll provider until 30 June 2027.
9. **Intercompany.** A services agreement between Kestrel Pay Ltd and Lumen starts on 1 January 2027 to recharge hosting and shared staff.
10. **Tax.** Lumen has €14.2m of tax losses carried forward. Portuguese rules can restrict their use after a change of ownership; Hartwell Moss will advise by Day 30.
11. **Budget.** Jonas Berg adds Lumen to the FY2027 budget for the full year 2027 before it goes to the board on 9 December 2026.
