# Month-end close checklist

Owner: Tom Whitfield (Financial Controller). Last updated 28 September 2026 with the dates for the September 2026 close, which is also the Q3 close.

This checklist covers Kestrel Pay Ltd and Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd. Every task has one owner. If you can't make a deadline, tell Tom before it passes, not after.

## How the calendar works

- WD1 is the first working day of the new month. WD0 is the last working day of the month being closed; WD-1 and WD-2 are the two working days before it.
- The close is complete on **WD6**, when the ledger is locked.
- Management accounts go out on **WD8**.
- Flash revenue goes to the exec team on **WD3**.

## Rules

- No journal goes into a locked period without Tom Whitfield's approval and a reason in the period close log.
- Journals over £100,000 need a second reviewer: Tom Whitfield, or Daniel Reyes for Tom's own journals.
- Don't post accruals under £5,000 unless they recur every month.
- Interns may prepare schedules and reconciliations. They may not post journals.
- The monthly safeguarding return to the FCA is separate from this checklist. It is due within 15 business days of month end; see the safeguarding reconciliation procedure.
