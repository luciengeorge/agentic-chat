# New Starter Checklist

Owner: Rachel Obi, HR Business Partner. Last updated 14 July 2026.

Use this list for every new employee, contractor and intern. The hiring manager owns it. Tick each item in the new starter task in Kestrel People so the People team can see progress. Anything not done by the date shown gets chased by Rachel Obi.

## Before day one (hiring manager, recruiter, People team)

- [ ] Signed contract and right-to-work check complete. Owner: Aisha Rahman, Recruiter. Due: offer accepted plus 5 working days.
- [ ] Background and credit checks passed. Owner: Aisha Rahman. Every role needs them, because Kestrel is FCA-regulated. Roles in Finance, Payments and Compliance also need a criminal records check.
- [ ] Start date confirmed with Ella Brooks for a desk and a pass. Due: 5 working days before the start date.
- [ ] Laptop and accounts requested from IT. Owner: hiring manager, through the IT request form. Due: 10 working days before the start date, so Tariq Aziz's team can ship it to Lisbon or Dublin in time.
- [ ] Access requests raised for the systems the role needs (for example `kp-deploy` for engineers, the finance system for Finance). Owner: hiring manager. Access is granted on day one, not before.
- [ ] Buddy chosen from the same team, and told. Owner: hiring manager.
- [ ] First-week calendar sent, including the IT setup session and the welcome session. Owner: hiring manager.

## Day one

- [ ] Collect pass and laptop from reception (London) or the office coordinator (Lisbon, Dublin).
- [ ] IT setup session, Mondays at 10:00 with Tariq Aziz: sign in, set up multi-factor authentication, enrol the laptop, install the company apps.
- [ ] Welcome session with the People team, Mondays at 14:00.
- [ ] Meet your manager and your buddy.
- [ ] Read the Employee Handbook and the Office Guide.

## First week

- [ ] Read the Code of Conduct and confirm it in Kestrel People.
- [ ] Complete security awareness training.
- [ ] Complete data protection training.
- [ ] Add your bank details, emergency contact and pension choices in Kestrel People.
- [ ] Book your first month's office days in Kestrel Desks.
- [ ] Engineers: read the Platform Architecture Overview, the Deploy Runbook and the On-call Runbook.

## First 30 days

- [ ] Complete the financial crime training (anti-money laundering, sanctions, fraud). This is a regulatory requirement: it must be done within 30 days of joining.
- [ ] Agree objectives for probation with your manager, written in Kestrel People.
- [ ] End of month 1 probation check-in with your manager.
- [ ] Sales and Customer Success: shadow at least 3 customer calls and read the Sales Playbook.

## 60 to 90 days

- [ ] Engineers: shadow one on-call week before joining a rota. You cannot join a primary rota before day 60.
- [ ] End of month 3 probation review. The manager confirms the outcome in Kestrel People by the last day of probation. If the manager does not confirm it, the People team contacts them; probation is not passed automatically.
- [ ] Give feedback on your onboarding in the 90 day survey.

## Interns

Interns follow the same list, except:

- they have no access to Restricted folders, even if their team does, unless their manager asks Marco Bianchi in writing;
- they do not join on-call rotas;
- their end-of-placement review replaces the month 3 probation review.
