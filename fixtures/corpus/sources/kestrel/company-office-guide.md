# Office Guide

Owner: Ella Brooks, Office Manager. Last updated 7 September 2026. Questions to reception@kestrelpay.example.

## 2. London: Harrow Yard

### 2.1 Getting there

Old Street station (Northern line) is a 6 minute walk. Liverpool Street (Elizabeth line, Central line and national rail) is 12 minutes. There are 40 bike spaces and 6 showers in the basement, entered from the side door on Paul's Alley.

### 2.2 Floors

| Floor | Teams and rooms |
|---|---|
| 3 | Reception, Customer Success, Sales, Engineering and Product, meeting rooms 3.1 to 3.6, main kitchen |
| 4 | Finance, Legal, People, Operations, Exec, boardroom 4.1, meeting rooms 4.2 to 4.4, quiet room |

The east side of floor 4 (Finance, Legal, People and Exec) is pass-restricted. Visitors to floor 4 must be escorted at all times.

### 2.3 Desks and rooms

Book a desk in Kestrel Desks by 18:00 the day before. Tuesday and Wednesday are the busiest days, so book early. Meeting rooms are booked in Outlook; release a room you no longer need. The boardroom (4.1) is booked through the Exec assistant only.

## 3. Lisbon and Dublin

Lisbon is home to a large part of Engineering, including most of Payments Engineering, and to Operations teams that cover weekend Support. Dublin hosts Operations, Sales and Customer Success for Ireland, part of Engineering, and the Kestrel Pay Europe compliance team. Both offices use the same Kestrel Desks booking and the same pass system as London, so your pass works in all three offices once it has been enabled for that site. Ask the local office coordinator at lisbon.office@kestrelpay.example or dublin.office@kestrelpay.example.

### 3.1 Dublin move, December 2026

The Dublin team, part of Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd, moves to the 2nd floor, Block C, Harcourt Quay, Dublin 2, in early December 2026. The new floor has 110 desks, against 73 people in Dublin at the end of June. Until the move, keep using Merrion Row. The move date and new building hours will be added here once the fit-out is finished.

## 4. Passes and visitors

- You get your pass on your first day from reception. If you lose it, tell Ella Brooks the same day. The first replacement is free; after that it is £10.
- Do not let anyone follow you through a pass gate, even a colleague.
- Register visitors by 16:00 the day before at reception@kestrelpay.example, with their name, company and host. Visitors sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and are escorted by their host.
- Contractors working on the building must sign in and out every day.

## 6. Health and safety

### 6.1 Fire

The London fire alarm is tested every Wednesday at 10:30; a short ring is the test. A continuous alarm means leave the building by the nearest stairs, not the lifts, and go to the assembly point at the far end of Harrow Yard, by the bollards.

| Floor | Fire warden |
|---|---|
| 3 | Ella Brooks, Kofi Mensah |
| 4 | Maya Levi |

### 6.2 First aid

First aiders in London are Ella Brooks, Hannah Kim and Luis Ortega. First aid kits are next to every kitchen, and the defibrillator is at reception on floor 3. Report any accident at work to Ella Brooks the same day, however small.

## 7. Kitchens and post

- Kitchens are on every floor in London. Fruit arrives on Monday and Thursday, and there is breakfast on Tuesdays from 08:30.
- Label food in the fridges. Fridges are cleared every Friday at 16:00.
- Post arrives at reception at about 10:00 and 14:00. Personal parcels are allowed, but please collect them within 2 days.
