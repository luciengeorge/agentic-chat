# Management accounts: August 2026

**Kestrel Pay Ltd and Kestrel Pay Europe Ltd, combined, in GBP. Unaudited.** Issued on WD8, Thursday 10 September 2026, by Tom Whitfield (Financial Controller) with Jonas Berg (FP&A Manager). Reviewed by Daniel Reyes (CFO).

## 1. Summary

**Table 1: Income summary, £000**

| Line | August 2026 | August budget | Variance | July 2026 | Q3 to date | Q3 to date budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 13,401 | 13,650 | (249) | 13,520 | 26,921 | 27,350 |
| Gross profit | 7,746 | 7,990 | (244) | 7,815 | 15,561 | 16,000 |
| Gross margin | 57.8% | 58.5% | (0.7) pts | 57.8% | 57.8% | 58.5% |
| Operating costs | (7,156) | (7,230) | 74 | (7,114) | (14,270) | (14,460) |
| EBITDA | 590 | 760 | (170) | 701 | 1,291 | 1,540 |

- **Revenue** was £249k (1.8%) below budget. UK card volume fell more than usual in August: Checkout payment volume was £1.58bn against £1.66bn in July. The 47-minute Checkout outage on 17 August (INC-2026-0817) cost about £7,900 of revenue, and the service credits to Checkout Pro merchants (about £2,650) are netted off August revenue. Payouts was flat on July; the two payroll bureaus went live on 1 September.
- **Gross margin** was 57.8%. Two merchant fraud cases added £61k of losses, of which £12k has been recovered so far.
- **Operating costs** were £74k under budget. Staff costs were under because headcount is 597 against a budget of 611; professional fees were over, mainly Carrow Finch's fees on the Dublin office lease (£85k).
- **Year to date** (January to August) revenue is £104.0m against a budget of £104.9m.

## 4. Cash

Cash fell by £0.2m in August, to £70.8m. EBITDA of £0.6m was more than used by capital spending of £0.9m (mostly capitalised development) and lease payments of £0.2m, with net interest of £0.1m received and a £0.2m inflow from working capital. Customer funds in the safeguarding accounts are not included.
