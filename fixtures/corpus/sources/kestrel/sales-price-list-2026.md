All prices exclude VAT. UK merchants are billed in GBP. EU merchants (Ireland, Portugal and other EEA countries) are billed in EUR at the EUR prices shown. Percentages are of the transaction value.

## 4. Contract terms

- Monthly rolling plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
- 12-month plans renew for another 12 months unless the merchant gives 30 days' notice before the end of the term.
- Ending a 12-month plan early costs the remaining monthly fees for the term, capped at £300 / €350 per plan.
- A merchant can move from a monthly plan to the 12-month version of the same plan at any time. The new term starts on the day of the change.
- There are no set-up fees on any plan.
