## 1. Recommendation

We recommend that the board approves the acquisition of the whole share capital of Lumen Ledger, Lda ("Lumen") for an enterprise value of £86m on a cash-free, debt-free basis, and authorises the CEO and CFO to sign the share purchase agreement (SPA) on the terms in section 5.

If the board approves on 20 October, we will sign on Friday 23 October and complete on Tuesday 1 December 2026.

The price is the same as the non-binding offer the board approved on 22 July 2026. Due diligence found nothing that justifies a change. The two open items in section 6 are covered by an escrow and a specific indemnity.

## 2. Why Lumen

- Lumen makes cloud bookkeeping and invoicing software for small businesses in Portugal and Spain. Its invoicing module is certified by the Portuguese tax authority (AT), and it produces the SAF-T files every Portuguese business has to submit.
- Our merchants keep asking for bookkeeping inside Kestrel Accounts. In the Q2 merchant survey, 41% of Kestrel Accounts customers said they pay for a separate bookkeeping tool and would switch to one built in. Building our own would take 18 to 24 months and would still need AT certification.
- Lumen gives us a base in Iberia. We have 3,940 merchants in Portugal and 1,120 in Spain. Lumen has 6,200 paying customers, and only about 300 of them use Kestrel today.
- Lumen's 74 staff work in Lisbon (66) and Porto (8). Our Lisbon office already has 172 people, so the two teams can share one site from 2027.

## 4. Valuation

At £86m we pay 10.3 times current ARR. European SME software deals in the last 24 months were done at 8 to 12 times ARR (Hartwell Moss comparables, appendix B of their report). Our discounted cash flow on the base case, without synergies, gives a range of £79m to £97m (discount rate 14%, terminal growth 3%). With synergies the base case rises to £112m.

| Synergy | 2027 | 2028 | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bookkeeping add-on sold in Kestrel Accounts (ARR) | £1.2m | £3.9m | Medium |
| Kestrel Accounts sold to Lumen customers (ARR) | £0.6m | £2.6m | Medium |
| Shared Lisbon office (cost, per year) | £0.3m | £0.6m | High |
| Lumen hosting moved to our Ashgrove contract (cost, per year) | £0.2m | £0.5m | High |

That is £6.5m of revenue synergies (ARR) by the end of 2028 and £1.1m a year of cost savings from 2028. We have not paid for synergies: the price sits inside the stand-alone DCF range.

## 7. Risks

- **Integration.** Lumen's product has to join Kestrel Accounts without slowing either roadmap. The integration plan sets out a 100-day programme led by the CFO, with its own budget of £3.1m in 2027.
- **Earn-out behaviour.** An ARR earn-out can tempt sellers to discount. The SPA defines ARR to exclude discounts above 20% and contracts shorter than 12 months.
- **Spain.** Spanish e-invoicing rules (Verifactu) need product work in 2027. Lumen has a plan and two engineers on it.
- **Currency.** Lumen earns and spends in euros. The price is fixed in sterling, so we will not hedge it.
- **Exec time.** Integration overlaps with FY2027 planning. Jonas Berg will add Lumen to the FY2027 budget once the deal is signed and he has been briefed.

## 8. Costs and confidentiality

Deal costs so far are £512k against the £650k due diligence budget approved in July. We expect £1.4m in total, including SPA work and the insurance premium. The costs sit in restricted cost centre 9901, which only the CFO and the Financial Controller can see. Under FRS 102 we hold them on the balance sheet until completion and add them to the cost of the acquisition; if the deal does not go ahead, they are written off in that month.

Helen Marsh keeps the insider list. It has 8 names at Kestrel: the four executives, Tom Whitfield (for finance integration planning) and our three non-executive directors. Nobody else is to be briefed before signing. We will tell staff and customers on completion day, not at signing, unless the deal leaks first.
