# 2026 Holiday Calendar

Published by Ella Brooks (Office Manager) and Marco Bianchi (Head of People). Last updated 6 January 2026.

Each office follows its own public holidays. You get the public holidays of the office in your employment contract, wherever you happen to be working that day. Public holidays are on top of your annual leave: 27 days in the UK, and 25 days in Portugal and Ireland.

## 1. London (England)

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| 3 April | Friday | Good Friday |
| 6 April | Monday | Easter Monday |
| 4 May | Monday | Early May bank holiday |
| 25 May | Monday | Spring bank holiday |
| 31 August | Monday | Summer bank holiday |
| 25 December | Friday | Christmas Day |
| 28 December | Monday | Boxing Day (substitute day, as 26 December is a Saturday) |

8 days in total.

## 4. Winter closure

All three offices close from Tuesday 29 December to Thursday 31 December 2026. These 3 days are given by Kestrel and do not come out of your annual leave. Offices reopen on Monday 4 January 2027 (Friday 1 January is New Year's Day in all three countries).

On-call rotas, Customer Success support and payments operations keep running through the closure. If you work on a closure day, you get the day back as time off in lieu, to be taken by 31 March 2027.

## 5. Busy periods to avoid for leave

These are not blackout dates, but managers may decline leave requests in them:

| Team | Period | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | First 5 working days after each quarter end | Quarter-end close |
| Payments Engineering | Last 2 business days of each month | Payroll payout peak |
| Customer Success | 23 November to 4 December 2026 | Black Friday and Cyber Monday volumes |
| Engineering | 18 December 2026 to 4 January 2027 | Change freeze: on-call cover must be kept |

Book leave in Kestrel People. The Leave Policy in /HR/Policies sets out notice periods and carry-over.
