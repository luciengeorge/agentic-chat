# Procurement Process

**Harbour Health.** Owner: George Evans, Procurement Lead. Approved by Nina Osei, Operations Director, and Fatima Ali, Finance Officer. Version 1.4, last updated 3 June 2026.

This process applies whenever anyone at Harbour buys goods or services, from printer paper to a building contract. It sits alongside the Delegated Financial Authority in the Finance folder, which sets who can approve how much.

## 1. Principles

- **No PO, no pay.** Raise a purchase order before you order. Suppliers are told that Harbour does not pay invoices without a PO number.
- Use a supplier from the approved supplier list where one can supply what you need.
- Get value for money, not just the lowest price: think about delivery, quality, support and running costs.
- Declare any personal link to a supplier to George Evans before you take part in a purchase.

## 2. Thresholds

All values are the total value of the order excluding VAT. For contracts that run more than a year, use the total over the whole contract.

| Value | Quotes needed | Who approves |
|---|---|---|
| Under £500 | 1 quote, or a catalogue price | Clinic manager or budget holder |
| £500 to £4,999 | 3 written quotes | Clinic manager up to £1,000; Procurement Lead above £1,000 |
| £5,000 to £24,999 | 3 written quotes and a short value-for-money note | Operations Director |
| £25,000 and above | Formal tender run by the Procurement Lead | Board |

Do not split an order to get under a threshold. Orders to the same supplier for the same need within 3 months count as one order.

## 3. Raising a purchase order

1. Fill in the purchase request form in the Operations folder: what, why, supplier, value, budget code.
2. Attach the quotes required by section 2.
3. Send it to the approver in section 2. They approve by email.
4. George Evans raises the PO in the finance system within 2 working days of approval and sends it to the supplier.
5. When the goods or service arrive, the requester confirms receipt to finance@harbourhealth.example. Finance pays only once the receipt is confirmed.

Purchases under £100 from approved suppliers can be made on a Harbour purchase card instead of a PO. Purchase cards are held by each clinic manager and by George Evans, with a limit of £1,000 a month per card.

## 4. New suppliers

To add a supplier to the approved list, send George Evans:

- the supplier's company details and VAT number;
- their bank details on headed paper, confirmed by phone to a number we already hold or find independently, never one given in the same email;
- proof of insurance where they work on our sites;
- a completed data protection questionnaire if they will handle any staff personal data.

New suppliers are approved within 5 working days. Changes to a supplier's bank details always need a phone check by Finance before the next payment.

## 5. Tenders

For £25,000 and above, George Evans runs a tender:

1. Write the specification with the requester and agree scoring criteria before any bids are opened.
2. Invite at least 3 suppliers, or advertise openly for £100,000 and above.
3. Give suppliers at least 15 working days to respond.
4. Score bids with a panel of 3, including someone outside the requesting team.
5. Record the decision and the scores in the Operations folder.

## 6. Payment terms

Harbour's standard payment terms are 30 days from a valid invoice. Finance runs supplier payments on Tuesdays and Fridays. Small local suppliers can ask for 14-day terms; George Evans decides.
