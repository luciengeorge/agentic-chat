## 3. Staff training afternoons

Every clinic closes from 13:00 on the second Wednesday of each month for staff training, except in August and December. In 2026 the remaining training afternoons are 9 September, 14 October and 11 November.

## 6. Changing opening hours

Only the Operations Director can change a clinic's opening hours. Planned changes are announced to staff at least 4 weeks ahead and posted on the clinic door and website at least 2 weeks ahead. If a clinic must close at short notice, for example because cover fell below the minimum in the Staff Rota Policy, the duty manager:

1. tells Nina Osei, or the operations on-call phone out of hours;
2. puts the closure message on the clinic's phone line;
3. asks David Cho's IT team to post the notice on the website;
4. redirects visitors to the nearest open clinic.
