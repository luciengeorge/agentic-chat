# Local models against OpenAI and Cohere: 2026-10-20

Measured on an Apple M4 with 32 GB, under other work (load average 10 to 15 throughout), by `pnpm eval` (hosted)
and `pnpm local:eval` (local) over the 46 golden questions. The runs' own reports sit beside this one.

## Retrieval (`--retrieval-only`)

| Run | Embeddings | Reranker | Rewrites | recall@10 | MRR | NDCG@10 | Gates | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| `2026-10-20-retrieval-only` | OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small@1536` | Cohere `rerank-v4.0-fast` | gpt-6-luna | 1.000 | 0.944 | 0.945 | pass | $0.0028 |
| `2026-10-20-retrieval-only-norewrite` | OpenAI | Cohere | none | 1.000 | 0.944 | 0.947 | pass | $0.00002 |
| `2026-10-20-retrieval-only-norewrite-local` | nomic-embed-text-v1.5 (768, padded) | bge-reranker-base, int8, transformers.js | none | 1.000 | **0.887** | **0.900** | pass | $0 |

The fair comparison is the last two rows: neither rewrites, since there's no local chat model to rewrite with.
Rewrites made no difference to the hosted numbers today.

- **Recall holds.** Every labelled page is still in the top 10 for all 40 answer questions. MRR drops 0.057.
- **Gates all pass locally:** no leak, no cross-tenant chunk, every abstain case empty-handed (retrieval decides
  those), no errors. The ACL is in the database, so the models can't change it.
- **Where the rank drops.** Six questions lose rank 1, and one gains it:
  - `kpc-2025-0142-subject` (rank 4) and `kp-4031-retry` (rank 2) are lexical traps. The identifiers in them,
    KPC-2025-0142 and KP-4031, are the kind of string a small cross-encoder doesn't weigh the way Cohere does.
  - `expenses-london-hotel` (rank 2): policy v1 outranks v2, the conflicting-sources trap.
  - `ocr-incident-duplicates` (rank 2) and `filing-sg-2025-02` (rank 2).
  - `ocr-msa-received` goes from rank 2 to rank 1.
- **By trap** (local MRR against hosted): lexical 0.88 against 1.00; conflicting sources 0.75 against 0.88;
  fan-out 0.90 against 1.00. ACL leak, OCR, tables, tenant isolation and prompt injection are the same.
- **The int8 reranker isn't deterministic across batch sizes.** Its activations are quantized per batch, so a
  pair's score depends on the pairs scored with it. In batches of eight, MRR was 0.899. One at a time, the default
  now, it was 0.887. Same weights, same candidates.

## Latency (`pnpm local:bench`, 10 questions, `2026-10-20-bench.json`)

| Stage | Median |
|---|---:|
| Whole retrieval | 3.2 s |
| Query embedding (LM Studio, nomic) | 30 ms |
| Database: searches, ACL decision, audit | 94 ms |
| Rerank, 30 pairs (int8, CPU, in the app's process) | 3.0 s |
| Reranker load, once per process | 0.6 to 2.6 s |

- **The reranker is nearly all of it.** The eval's local retrieval had a median of 2.7 s a question and a p90 of
  5.1 s. On one question's 30 pairs, one pair per pass took 2.3 s, and batches of 4 to 30 took 4.6 to 6.8 s
  (a batch pads every pair to the longest in it). One per pass is now the default (`RERANK_BATCH_SIZE`).
- **Hosted latency isn't comparable from these runs.** The eval waits 6.5 s between Cohere calls (a trial key's
  rate limit), and that wait is inside its per-question times.
- **Embedding the corpus** (`pnpm local:db`): 5,708 chunks, 2.17M tokens, in 561 s, about 3,900 tokens a second.
  OpenAI took 37 s for the same text.

## Egress (`pnpm local:egress-check`, `2026-10-20-egress.json`)

- **The network.** On the `airgap` Compose network (`internal: true`), every attempt to leave failed within 4 to
  15 ms. DNS for api.openai.com, api.cohere.com and huggingface.co didn't resolve. TCP to 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8 got
  "network is unreachable". host.docker.internal:1234 (LM Studio on the host) failed too. The database on the same
  network answered in 6 ms.
- **The process.** A golden question (`expenses-meals`, as leo.martin) went through the app's retrieval on local
  models under the egress monitor. Policy v2 came back at rank 2, and the monitor refused no connection: it
  allowed only `localhost:5432` and `127.0.0.1:1234`. The eval runs above ran under the monitor too, also with
  nothing refused.
- **Hosted clients, refused.** With no retries, OpenAI's embeddings failed in 18 ms and Cohere's rerank in 5 ms.
  With the app's retry settings, the same refusal took 6.4 s for OpenAI (3 attempts) and 62 s for Cohere
  (6 attempts, backing off from 2 s). So "fails fast" holds at the socket and not through a retry policy. This is
  why local mode never builds a hosted client.
